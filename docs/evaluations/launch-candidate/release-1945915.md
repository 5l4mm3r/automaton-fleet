- The runtime sends it: `founder/mind.ts` posts `mind.report` (`wakeOn`, `reviewAt`, outcome, tools) via `POST /v1/economy`
  at the end of every routed turn (best-effort, dropped on failure). The controller (v62 `fleet_econ_mind_report`, op class
  `planning`) writes `fleet_agent_mind_reports`; v75 reads it. Present in b60e262 (v62 = 3b456cd, an ancestor).

  re-check (`HIBERNATION_SAFETY_MS`, anchored to the last turn's time and restored across restarts); that wake runs a turn
  that reports again. Consecutive reports are at most ~24 h + one thinking slot + the turn apart.
