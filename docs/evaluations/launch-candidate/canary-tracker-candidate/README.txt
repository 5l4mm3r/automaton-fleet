UK Landlord Compliance Tracker (England) - v2 (Excel .xlsx; works in Excel, LibreOffice, Google Sheets)
Enter dates (dd/mm/yyyy) in the white cells; due dates and OVERDUE / DUE SOON / OK flags calculate automatically (200 rows). Overwrite the sample row.
Intervals used (checked against gov.uk):
- Gas safety check: every 12 months (gov.uk private-renting/your-landlords-safety-responsibilities)
- Electrical inspection (EICR): at least every 5 years (gov.uk electrical safety standards guidance)
- EPC: valid for 10 years (gov.uk selling-a-home/energy-performance-certificates)
This is an organising tool, not legal advice. See gov.uk "Guide to the Renters' Rights Act".
