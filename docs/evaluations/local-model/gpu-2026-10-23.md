## Why the correction was not delivered

Step log (`mind-log.jsonl`): turn 4 read the request and rewrote the files; turn 5 rebuilt the PDF and tried to review and
redeliver; turn 6 went to opportunity / venture work and slept. Nothing woke it for the remaining 41 minutes.

Platform defects (repaired in this change set):

1. **A stale review was returned as "already done".** The continuation guard (a completed external action of the carried turn
   is never repeated) matched the review of the REVISED `order/checklist.pdf` to the original review by its arguments alone
   and returned the original result. The redelivery then carried that review and was refused `FLEET_REVIEW_STALE`.
   Repair: the done record keeps the state (size, modification time) of the files a call names; a call on changed files runs.
2. **The revision was not owed work.** After the first delivery the order was no longer "delivery owed"; the buyer's request
   was only a conversation state (`acknowledged` once answered). The brief's objective reverted to the agent's own goal, the
   next move to opportunity work, and the declared sleep waited for a change that never came. Repair (v112): a buyer request
   after the latest sent delivery, unresolved, is `revisionOwed`; it becomes the objective and the next move (build, a NEW
   review, `wallet deliver` with `resendReason` and `after`, or resolve if nothing more is owed); a turn that ends asleep
   while a delivery or revision is owed is looked at again after 15 minutes (one turn, then waiting again; not a block).
3. **The procedure was never written.** `recordProcedure` read the deliver call's top-level arguments; the wallet tool nests
   them under `args`, so it found no paths. Its tests used the wrong shape. Repair: the nested arguments are read; the tests
   use the real shape (the procedure, item lookup and loop guard alike).
4. **The document itself was not kept.** The evidence kept only the files' hashes; the PDF could not be inspected after the
   run. Repair: the workspace's files (not `.tmp` renders; regular files, ≤ 5 MB, ≤ 60) are copied into the evidence.

Model weaknesses (9B), shown by its calls — not repaired, measured again next run:

- It told the buyer "I've updated the checklist … please see the revised PDF" **before** any revised delivery.
- Review arguments: `subject`, `verdict` and the `criteria` shape were wrong 7 times; in turn 3 it read `tool_help` and
  recovered, in turn 5 it did not.
- The resend omitted `resendReason` / `after` (`FLEET_OPERATION_EXISTS` ×2).
- Turn 1 tried `web_search` (not configured, ×3) and research before turning to the order.
